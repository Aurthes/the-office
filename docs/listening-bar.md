# Listening bar and observation lounge

The main deck has two independent cabins. **Observation lounge** keeps its sofas,
relaxing chairs and coffee table for watching the stars. **Listening bar** occupies
the neighbouring cabin across the ring's wraparound boundary; it remains beside
the lounge as the ship gains sectors. The normal public-cabin allocator reserves
this space. A project previously using that bay moves to an available work cabin;
its members and project membership remain intact.

The bar contains the counter, three high stools in front, speakers, bottles and
the turntable on its own side table. Clicking the counter or a stool uses the
standard zoom and object information card. Clicking the turntable opens the same
music player as before, with the existing local and NetEase libraries.

The robot bartender lives in the bar and retains its wash, pour, wipe and inventory
routine. Shared layout dimensions keep the rendered counter, stools and side table
aligned with collision checks. The staff lane stays behind the counter; crew and
companions pass along the front aisle. The lounge and other cabins retain their
existing furniture and behaviour.
